
# CHOG SOULS — Quick Demo Guide

## Setup (Before the Demo)

1. Start the backend: `cd backend && uvicorn app.main:app --reload`
2. Start the frontend: `npm run dev`
3. Open `http://localhost:5173` in Chrome.
4. **Optional:** Clear `localStorage` for a fresh state.

## The Flow (3 minutes)

### Step 1 — Landing (10s)

Say: **"Most NFTs are static. This one remembers you."**

Click **AWAKEN A SOUL**.

### Step 2 — Dashboard (15s)

Point at:

- The **purple Chog avatar**
- The **archetype** ("The Loyal Trickster")
- The **rarity badge** (UNCOMMON)
- The **stats**
- The **🔥 DAY 1** streak badge

### Step 3 — Talk (30s)

Click **TALK**. Send:

> "Do you trust Bob?"

Show the AI response. Point out:

- The Chog avatar pulses
- A sound effect plays
- The response is **in character** (references loyalty, memory)

### Step 4 — Quest (30s)

Click **QUEST**. Point out the **weekday** and **scenario**.

Click **RETURN IT**.

Show:

- The AI-generated narrative
- The stat changes
- The memory created

### Step 5 — Arena (40s)

Click **ARENA**.

Wait for NOVA's taunt to load. Read it aloud.

Click **START BATTLE**.

Show:

- The battle score
- The AI narrative
- NOVA's post-battle reaction
- The sound effect

### Step 6 — Level Up (20s)

Battle again until you hit a level up.

Show:

- The sparkle animation
- The **LEVEL X** banner
- The sound effect

### Step 7 — Leaderboard (20s)

Click **RANKS**.

Point at:

- **Your rank**
- The 9 rival Souls with unique avatars
- **Click any rival** to see their profile

### Step 8 — Share Card (20s)

Go to Dashboard. Click **SHARE SOUL**.

Show the generated card. Click **DOWNLOAD PNG**.

## Killer Demo Moments

1. **AI responds in character** — proves the tech works.
2. **Sparkle level-up banner** — visual delight.
3. **NOVA's taunt** — the world feels alive.
4. **Share card generation** — viral potential.
5. **Daily quest** — retention concept in one screen.

## Fallback Plan

**If the mainnet import fails:**

- Use **🎲 TRY A DEMO CHOG** on the Import page.
- 6 pre-built Chogs load instantly.

**If the AI is slow:**

- Talk to Giga, don't wait.
- Keep the demo under 4 minutes.

**If everything breaks:**

- Open DevTools → Console → `localStorage.clear()` → reload.
- Demo Mode loads automatically.
